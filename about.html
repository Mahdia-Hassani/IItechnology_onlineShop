About II Technology

A small idea that grew into Afghanistan's go-to spot for honest tech.

Our Story

II Technology started in 2021 as a small kiosk in Kabul selling phone accessories. Today we are a growing team that helps thousands of customers every year find original, well-priced gadgets — from AirPods and smart watches to full gaming setups.

We believe people deserve access to real technology at a fair price, with someone who can actually explain it to them.
